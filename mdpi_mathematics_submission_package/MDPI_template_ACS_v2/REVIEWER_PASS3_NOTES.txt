[P3A] §7.3 — Subsection title contradicted its own body text
  TEXT WAS: \subsection{The HYP paradox: not resolved at 20 seeds}
  PROBLEM: Body text says "The non-significant HYP result with 20 seeds
   strengthens confidence that this is not merely a power limitation,
   but rather a genuine absence of a detectable effect at the superclass
   level under this protocol." The statistical question IS resolved
   (genuine absence confirmed). The title "not resolved" implied the
   opposite — a reviewer would flag the direct contradiction between
   title and body.
  DISTINCTION: Statistically resolved (genuine null confirmed at 20 seeds)
   ≠ Mechanistically explained (four candidate explanations listed but
   not proven). The title should reflect the statistical reality.
  FIX APPLIED: "The HYP paradox: no detectable gain at the superclass
   level" — accurately states the result without claiming resolution of
   the mechanistic question. ✓

  power concern (hyp 1) while asserting the primary explanation is a
  real null. Defensible as written; a pedantic reviewer might query it
  but the phrasing "not merely" and "strengthens confidence" are
  appropriately hedged.

  λ_LAUC), so both weights change together." The reference loss weights

  Group C uses a different parameterisation (w_fused changes). This is
  correctly disclosed as an entangled variation; no fix needed.
