=== CRITICAL ERRORS (scientific correctness) ===

[C1] Discussion §Q2, line ~405:
  "Even the demographic-only contrast (demo−none) shows BH-significant effects
   for NORM (p_BH=0.015) and MI (p_BH=0.011)"
  → DEMO-NONE per-class tests are EXPLICITLY EXPLORATORY (not in any BH-FDR
    family per §5 Statistical Analysis). Calling them "BH-significant" is
    wrong. The correct values are p_raw=0.004 (NORM) and p_raw=0.003 (MI)
    from Results §Per-class. Fix: remove p_BH labels; use p_raw.

[C2] Discussion §CD, line ~411:
  "The demo variant produces a slight negative trend in CD AUC (−0.0011,
   p_BH=0.211)"
  → DEMO-NONE per-class is exploratory. "p_BH" label is wrong. Should be
    "p_raw=0.211" (the value itself is fine; just the label is incorrect).

[C3] Fig6 caption vs body text:
  Caption: "demo+anthro exceeds none in 19 of 20 seeds"
  Main text §5.4: "fused head … positive for all 20 of 20 seeds" (this is
  for AUC_fused - AUC_ecg gap, not DA-NONE macro). These are different
  quantities and actually consistent — just needs a clearer narrative in
  body text so readers don't confuse the two counts.

=== HIGH PRIORITY (methodology / precision) ===

[H1] Table 4 caption + Statistics section:
  The per-class DA-NONE sub-family BH-FDR is post-hoc / secondary, not
  pre-specified. The protocol section only pre-declares the 3-test macro-AUC
  family. Table 4 caption must make clear this is a "secondary analysis."
  Statistics section (§5.7) should add one sentence about this sub-family.

[H2] Eq.(5) q_meta^2 note:
  Since h_m = q_meta * MLP(u_m) (Eq.4), and Eq.5 has 0.10*q_meta*W*h_m,
  the combined effective attenuation is 0.10*q_meta^2. The text says only
  "q_meta attenuation" which hides the quadratic guard. Add note:
  "Combined with the q_meta scaling applied to h_m in Eq.(4), the effective
   attenuation of the residual path at partial availability is q_meta^2."

[H3] Metadata encoding Eq.(3) is confusing:
  x_meta bundles 5 actual values + 3 missingness indicators (m_h,m_w,m_bmi)
  into one 8-dim vector, PLUS there's a separate 8-dim availability mask m.
  A reviewer will be confused: what are the 8 bits of the separate mask m
  when x_meta already contains 3 missingness indicators?
  Need one clarifying sentence: "x_meta combines imputed metadata values
  (age_z, sex01, height_z, weight_z, bmi_z) with derived missingness
  indicators (m_h, m_w, m_bmi); the separate 8-dim mask m flags whether
  each entry of x_meta was originally observed before imputation."

[H4] §4.2 Dual metadata encoder:
  "a 4-dim demographic slice and a 12-dim anthropometric slice" — these are
  AFTER concatenating values+masks, not raw feature dims. Clarify:
  "(2 values + 2 availability masks)" and "(6 values + 6 availability masks)."

[H5] Training dynamics text (line ~310):
  "Cross-seed SD narrows from 0.008–0.010 at epoch 1 to 0.001–0.001 at
   epoch 10" → "0.001–0.001" is redundant/odd. Change to "≈0.001 at epoch 10."

[H6] GLU description:
  "preserves linear gradient flow" — slightly imprecise. Dauphin's GLU
  allows gradient to pass through the non-gated linear branch; the sigmoid-
  gated branch attenuates gradients. Better: "allows a direct linear gradient
  path through the ungated branch."

[H7] Group D augmentation language:
  "The result confirms that the light augmentation block contributes a
   consistent positive effect and is not a neutral design choice." — With
  n=10 and raw p=0.0195 (exploratory, no BH correction), "confirms" is too
  strong. Replace with "suggests" or "provides preliminary evidence."

[H8] Conclusions line:
  "provides sufficient power to detect small effects such as the demographic
   gain (d_z=0.64)" — no formal power analysis is done. Replace "sufficient
   power" with "enough paired seeds."
