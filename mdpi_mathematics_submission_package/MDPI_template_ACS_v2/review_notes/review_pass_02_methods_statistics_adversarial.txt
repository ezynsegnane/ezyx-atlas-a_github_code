Reviewer pass 02 - Relecture methodes/statistiques, posture adversariale
Manuscrit: main_en.tex / source_snapshot
Date locale: 2026-05-19

Objectif:
Relire depuis zero en cherchant les failles qu'un reviewer methodologique/statistique utiliserait pour attaquer le papier.

1. Hypothese principale et famille confirmatoire
- Le papier affirme une famille confirmatoire pre-specifiee de 3 tests Group A. Sans plan date, preregistration, commit timestamp, notebook lock, ou supplement S1 inclus, "pre-specified" est vulnerable. Un reviewer peut demander: pre-specifie quand, ou, par qui?
- Le choix de limiter le FDR a 3 tests est defendable si vraiment predeclare, mais le manuscrit rapporte beaucoup d'autres p-values. Les sections abstract/conclusion doivent distinguer strictement confirmatory vs exploratory.
- Les per-class tests DA-NONE sont secondaires post-hoc. Ils sont utiles, mais le langage "reach significance" doit rester descriptif et rappeler le statut post-hoc.

2. Unite d'inference
- L'unite statistique est le seed, pas le patient. C'est coherent pour mesurer la variance d'entrainement, mais cela ne donne pas une incertitude population/patient.
- Les effets principaux sont tres petits (+0.0007 et +0.0019 macro-AUC). Avec un split fixe, l'effet peut etre stable par seed tout en restant fragile par cohorte, appareil, pays, age distribution, ou label noise.
- Les conclusions doivent dire "under this fixed PTB-XL split/protocol" partout ou le lecteur pourrait lire une generalisation clinique.

3. Wilcoxon exact
- Le manuscrit doit preciser comment les ties et zero differences sont traites. SciPy peut changer la methode selon zero_method, exact/asymptotic, et presence de ties.
- Les p-values sont parfois donnees comme `<0.001`, `approx 0.00001`, ou `approx 0.009`. Pour une revendication de precision statistique, donner exact p-values et adjusted p-values dans une table supplementaire.
- Le p minimum `2/2^20` est correct pour all same sign sans ties, mais il faut eviter de laisser croire que cela suffit pour "power" a priori.

4. Bootstrap
- Le bootstrap de 20 seed-level valeurs est un bootstrap sur runs, pas sur patients. Il ne capture pas l'incertitude due aux exemples test.
- Les patient-level probability arrays ne sont pas archivees; cela empeche un reviewer de recalculer AUC CIs, DeLong, calibration, subgroup analysis, or patient bootstrap.
- Si possible, fournir au moins seed-level predictions/probabilities anonymisees par record id ou un script qui les regenere.

5. Group F
- Group F est appele "confirmatory generalisation check". Le design ne teste que demo+anthro sur quatre test folds, 5 seeds chacun.
- Il ne reexecute pas none/demo/demo+anthro sur ces folds. Donc il ne montre pas que le gain metadata generalise; il montre seulement que l'absolu demo+anthro est plus eleve sur folds 2/3/7/8 que fold 10.
- L'expression "fold 10 is conservatively difficult" doit devenir "fold 10 was lower for this configuration than the four alternative folds tested".
- Les fold pairs changent aussi validation fold. Un reviewer peut demander si le gap vient du test fold, du val fold, de checkpoint selection, ou des distributions.

6. Missingness experiment
- Incoherence directe: texte/Data Availability disent demo+anthro masking report sur 10 seeds (2024-2033), mais Figure 4 caption et script indiquent "n=20 seeds". C'est une remarque critique.
- The none/demo traces are structural baselines, not empirical masking experiments over anthropometric variables. Calling them "across 20 seeds" may be misleading unless the plotted values truly use all 20 seed test outputs.
- Independent masking of height, weight, and BMI is not physiologically realistic because BMI is derived from height and weight. The text acknowledges this, but it should be in Limitations and perhaps Figure 4 caption.
- Because `q_meta` saturates when age/sex are present, the missingness robustness is not mainly "quality-gated"; it is missingness flags plus training dropout plus the model learning to ignore absent anthro.

7. Label construction and benchmark comparability
- The loader marks a superclass positive if any SCP code assigned to the class is present in `scp_codes`; no likelihood threshold. PTB-XL codes include likelihoods. The manuscript must document whether this exactly matches the Strodthoff superdiagnostic protocol.
- If not exact, comparisons to xresnet1d101 macro-AUC 0.928 are only contextual, not a direct baseline.
- The paper says "canonical benchmark" several times. A reviewer will check this.

8. Preprocessing/code provenance
- Manuscript line 169 says ECG signals are divided by 5.0. The provided `source_snapshot/eznx_loader_v2.py` reads WFDB, pads/truncates, transposes, and returns tensor, but no division by 5.0 appears.
- The manuscript describes out-of-range metadata filtering and median imputation, but the provided loader reads precomputed columns. The index-building script is absent.
- The source snapshot lacks the training loop, loss implementation, augmentation code, threshold tuning, exact Wilcoxon/statistics code, and missingness evaluator.
- `source_snapshot/requirements.txt` says "All 30 training runs" although the manuscript says 250 runs. This damages reproducibility credibility.

9. Architecture claims
- "EZNX-ATLAS-A" is positioned as quality-gated GLU fusion, but Group E says simple concatenation/additive/no residual/no dropout/no q all perform similarly. This is fine scientifically, but the narrative should pivot: the architecture is an instrument, not the novelty.
- The `none` variant still uses the fused head and gate with zero metadata dimensions. It is not an independent pure ECG backbone baseline. It is a metadata-disabled version of the same architecture.
- The ECG auxiliary head inside demo+anthro is not an independent ECG-only model; the manuscript notes this. Keep this caveat in figure/table labels too.

10. Exploratory groups
- B/C/E have n=10 and no FDR. Statements like "confirm robustness", "confirm ECG essential", "not a neutral design choice" are too strong.
- Group D p=0.002 is still exploratory and uncorrected across exploratory probes. It motivates confirmatory testing but should not be elevated.
- E8 trainmask is an extreme train/inference mismatch; useful negative control, but not proof that metadata must generally be available during training in all designs.

11. Clinical interpretation
- No calibration, decision curve, reclassification, sensitivity/specificity at operating points, subgroup fairness, or external cohort. The manuscript mostly says this, but the conclusion should be even less clinical.
- MI/STTC mechanisms are plausible but untested. Use "may reflect", "consistent with", not "provide".
- HYP paradox is well discussed; still, subclass-level LVH analysis would be the natural reviewer request.

Pass 02 verdict:
The statistical framing is strong but fragile at its boundaries. The paper should lower confirmatory language outside Group A, fix missingness seed-count inconsistency, document the analysis plan, provide exact reproducibility artifacts, and clarify label/preprocessing provenance before submission.
