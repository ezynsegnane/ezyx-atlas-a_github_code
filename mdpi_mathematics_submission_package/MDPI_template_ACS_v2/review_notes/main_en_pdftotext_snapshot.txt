                                   1. Introduction                                                                                     32


                                         Automatic interpretation of the 12-lead electrocardiogram is one of the most mature           33

                                   applications of clinical deep learning. Hannun et al. [1] reported cardiologist-level ar-           34

                                   rhythmia detection on ambulatory single-lead recordings; Ribeiro et al. [2] demonstrated            35

                                   large-scale automatic diagnosis from standard 12-lead ECGs; and Attia et al. [3] showed             36

                                   that a 12-lead CNN could screen for reduced left-ventricular ejection fraction. Siontis et          37

                                   al. [4] review these and related AI-ECG applications in cardiovascular disease management.          38

                                   On the public PTB-XL corpus [5], the canonical benchmark of Strodthoff et al. [6] places            39

                                   superclass macro-AUC at 0.928 ± 0.005 for a 1D-ResNet. More recent self-supervised sys-             40

                                   tems [7–9] improve selected ECG benchmarks, but they are waveform-centric and therefore             41

                                   do not directly answer how much structured patient metadata adds to PTB-XL superclass               42

                                   classification.                                                                                     43

                                         A natural next step is multimodal fusion with structured clinical metadata, since PTB-XL      44

                                   exposes age and sex for nearly all records and height/weight-derived anthropometrics for            45

                                   a smaller subset. Recent ECG-fusion works [10–13] show that ECG representations can                 46

                                   be combined with clinical variables, patient-characteristic modules, or engineered feature          47

                                   tables. However, our paper-by-paper audit (Tables 9–10) found that these studies differ in          48

                                   task, dataset, and metadata definition; their reported gains should therefore not be read           49

                                   as direct numerical baselines for PTB-XL superclass demographic/anthropometric fusion.              50

                                   The specific gap we target is narrower: seed-matched ablation of demographics versus                51

                                   anthropometrics, per-class decomposition, and inference-time missingness stress testing.            52

                                         We contribute a targeted remedy. Rather than proposing yet another ECG backbone,              53

                                   we design EZNX-ATLAS-A—a deliberately compact (3.95 M parameters) quality-gated                     54

                                   dual-branch architecture—and use it as an instrument to address six questions. We inten-            55

                                   tionally keep a compact residual backbone, rather than escalate simultaneously to a larger          56

                                   Transformer or foundation waveform encoder, so that measured gains are easier to attribute          57

                                   to the metadata pathway itself and so that 250 unique runs across six experimental groups           58

                                   remain computationally tractable:                                                                   59



                                   Q1. When, if ever, does structured clinical metadata detectably improve PTB-XL superclass           60

                                      classification, once seed variance is accounted for?                                             61


                                   Q2. Which type of metadata (demographic vs. anthropometric) helps which pathology                   62

                                      class?                                                                                           63


                                   Q3. Can a quality-gated fusion degrade gracefully as anthropometric metadata fields are             64

                                     withheld, remaining near the ECG-only and demographics-only baselines?                            65


                                   Q4. How do our measured effects compare with recent multimodal ECG reports once task,               66

                                      dataset, metadata definition, and statistical protocol are audited?                              67


                                   Q5. Are the reported gains robust to architectural variants (fusion mechanism, gating,              68

                                      residual path) and is ECG signal necessary?                                                      69


                                   Q6. Are the results specific to fold 10, or do they generalise across alternative data splits?      70



                                       Our answers, detailed in Sections 6–7, are: (Q1) both demographic-only and full-                71

                                   metadata variants show statistically significant gains with 20 paired seeds: demographics           72




                                                                                                https://doi.org/10.3390/math1010000

                                   pBH ≈0.009), while full metadata (demo+anthro minus none) yield ∆=+0.0019 macro-                   74

                                   AUC (95% paired CI [0.0014, 0.0023], pBH <0.001). (Q2) The class-wise effect of full               75

                                        Beyond the ECG application, this paper introduces a reusable statistical measurement          89

                                   pipeline for multimodal-ML ablations: exact two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests            90

                                   on seed-matched differences, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR control at q=0.05 across a pre-                91

                                   declared 3-test family (the three pairwise macro-AUC contrasts of Group A), and percentile         92

                                   bootstrap CIs for paired differences. This pipeline applies to any ablation study in which         93

                                   model variants are compared across repeated random seeds.                                          94




                                   2. Related Work                                                                                    95


                                   2.1. Supervised benchmarks on PTB-XL                                                               96


                                         Wagner et al. [5] released PTB-XL, a 21 799-record 12-lead corpus with SCP-ECG               97

                                   multi-label annotations aggregated into 5 superclasses, 23 sub-classes and 44 diagnos-             98

                                   tic statements, together with a stratified, patient-disjoint 10-fold partition. Strodthoff et      99

                                   al. [6] established the canonical benchmark, reporting 10-fold cross-validated macro-AUC           100

                                   0.928 ± 0.005 on super-diag with xresnet1d101. Nonaka and Seita [14] swept nine 1D-                101

                                   CNN backbones and found ResNet-18 competitive with deeper variants. The Bydgoszcz                  102

                                   group [15,16] studied entropy features and few-shot learning but used a non-standard               103

                                   70/15/15 split, making their numbers incomparable to the canonical benchmark. In the               104

                                   supervised PTB-XL benchmark papers audited here, we did not identify a seed-matched                105

                                   age/sex vs. anthropometric ablation with an explicit missing-metadata stress test.                 106



                                   2.2. Landmark clinical ECG models                                                                  107


                                        Hannun et al. [1] reached cardiologist-level rhythm classification on 91 232 single-lead      108

                                   ambulatory ECGs; Ribeiro et al. [2] generalised to 12-lead short recordings on a 2M-record         109

                                   Brazilian telehealth corpus. Attia et al. [3] showed a CNN could screen for LV ejection            110

                                   fraction ≤ 35% directly from the 12-lead ECG (AUC 0.93). Critically for the present paper,         111

                                   Attia et al. [17] demonstrated that age and sex are themselves recoverable from the raw ECG        112

                                   (age MAE 6.9 y, sex AUC 0.97), supporting the idea that demographic signal is already partly       113

                                   present in the waveform. Siontis et al. [4] identify this latent-demographic phenomenon as         114

                                   both an opportunity and a confounding risk.                                                        115



                                   2.3. Self-supervised and foundation ECG models                                                     116


                                        Contrastive and masked-modelling approaches—CLOCS [18], CPC-based pretrain-                   117

                                   ing [7], ST-MEM [19], the ECG joint-embedding predictive architecture (ECG-JEPA) [8], and          118

                                   preprint waveform foundation models such as HuBERT-ECG [9] and TolerantECG [20]—                   119




                                                                                               https://doi.org/10.3390/math1010000
Version May 19, 2026 submitted to Mathematics                                                                                  4 of 28




                                   study representation learning and robustness from waveform inputs alone, including                    120

                                   PTB-XL downstream evaluations in some cases. They are therefore adjacent to, but distinct             121

                                   from, the metadata-value question studied here. The non-peer-reviewed preprints in this               122

                                   paragraph are cited as contextual neighbouring work rather than as pre-specified baselines.           123



                                   2.4. Multimodal ECG + tabular fusion                                                                  124


                                        Explicit ECG + tabular or feature-fusion architectures have begun to appear, but they            125

                                   are not interchangeable baselines. Qiu et al. [10] fuse a transformer ECG encoder with                126

                                   clinical features for atrial-fibrillation recurrence prediction after ablation, outside PTB-          127

                                   XL. The preprint rECGnition_v2.0 system [11] studies self-attentive canonical fusion on               128

                                   MIT-BIH, INCARTDB and the European ST-T Database. Among PTB-XL-family studies,                        129

                                   Atwa et al. [12] (whose published title uses the abbreviation “PTB-X”, referring to the same          130

                                   PhysioNet corpus) is the closest published demographic-fusion comparator because it adds              131

                                   age and sex to CNN/VGG-style arrhythmia models; however, it reports accuracy-oriented                 132

                                   results across 2-, 5-, 10-, and 15-class tasks with augmentation rather than seed-matched             133

                                   macro-AUC on the canonical five-superclass split. In parallel, PTB-XL+ [21] augments PTB-             134

                                   XL with engineered Uni-G, 12SL and ECGDeli feature tables; Holloway et al. [13] compare               135

                                   raw PTB-XL signals with such PTB-XL+ feature representations. These feature tables are                136

                                   derived from the ECG itself rather than from EHR-like anthropometrics. We therefore                   137

                                   treat these papers as motivation and methodological context, not as direct numerical                  138

                                   comparators; the exact comparability audit is summarised in Tables 9–10.                              139



                                   2.5. Gated fusion mechanisms                                                                          140


                                        The Gated Linear Unit (GLU) of Dauphin et al. [22] provides a simple multiplicative              141

                                   gate h ⊙ σ (·) that preserves a direct linear gradient path through the ungated branch. The           142

                                   Gated Multimodal Unit [23] extends this to modality-level mixing. In medical imaging,                 143

                                   Chen et al. [24] use feature disentanglement plus gated fusion for brain-tumor segmentation,          144

                                   and Kim et al. [25] introduce reliability-aware gating for degraded or low-quality modalities.        145

                                   We use a GLU-based gate conditioned on an explicit scalar quality score qmeta derived from            146

                                   the metadata-availability mask.                                                                       147



                                   2.6. Missing modalities in clinical ML                                                                148


                                        Baltrušaitis et al. [26] survey the main settings of multimodal learning, including              149

                                   fusion and co-learning under incomplete modalities. HeMIS [27], SMIL [28], and mm-                    150

                                   Former [29] address missing modalities in imaging or general multimodal settings rather               151

                                   than ECG+tabular fusion. We study the adjacent ECG+tabular setting by coupling an                     152

                                   availability-mask-driven quality score with stochastic sample meta-dropout during train-              153

                                   ing.                                                                                                  154



                                   2.7. Clinical motivation for anthropometrics in ECG classification                                    155


                                        Classical ECG LVH criteria—Sokolow–Lyon [30], Cornell voltage/duration prod-                     156

                                   uct [31], Romhilt–Estes [32], and Peguero–Lo Presti [33]—depend on measured voltage                   157

                                   and are affected by body habitus. Left ventricular mass is itself routinely indexed to body           158

                                   surface area or height2.7 . A priori, these cardiology facts suggest that the HYP superclass          159

                                   could benefit from anthropometric metadata. More broadly, ECG amplitudes and repolari-                160

                                   sation measurements relevant to MI and STTC can also be influenced by torso conduction                161

                                   properties, lead geometry, and recording context [34,35], suggesting possible relevance               162

                                   of body-size priors across morphology-rich classes. Our per-class results in Section 6 are            163

                                   compatible with this broader relevance while not supporting a strong HYP-specific effect              164

                                   at the superclass level.                                                                              165

                                   3. Dataset and Preprocessing                                                                                      166


                                   3.1. PTB-XL 1.0.3                                                                                                 167


                                         We use PTB-XL version 1.0.3 on PhysioNet [5,36] at 100 Hz sampling rate (T =1000                            168

                                   samples, 10 s, 12 leads). Following [6], SCP-ECG diagnostic statements are mapped to the                          169

                                   5-class super-diagnostic task {NORM, MI, STTC, CD, HYP} using scp_statements.csv.                                 170

                                   In the implemented loader, a record is positive for a superclass if any diagnostic SCP                            171

                                   code assigned to that class is present in scp_codes; no additional likelihood threshold is                        172

                                   applied at load time, which is consistent with the standard superdiagnostic benchmark                             173

                                   label protocol [6]. The official stratified-by-label and patient-disjoint 10-fold partition is                    174

                                   used unchanged in the working index: folds 1–8 for training, fold 9 for validation, fold-10                       175

                                   for test, yielding 17,418 / 2,183 / 2,198 records, respectively (Table 5). These fold counts                      176

                                   sum to 21 799 records, matching the current PhysioNet v1.0.3 release after the duplicate                          177

                                   removals documented in its release notes.                                                                         178

                                   3.5. Preprocessing                                                                                     213


                                        Signals are loaded with wfdb at 100 Hz, padded or truncated to 1000 time samples,                 214

                                   transposed to channel-first shape (12, T ), cast to float32, and divided by 5.0 before entering        215

                                   the model. Metadata normalisation (z-scoring) and out-of-range conversion to missing are               216

                                   performed during index construction (before training): age 0–120 years (PTB-XL records                 217

                                   with age >89 may be encoded as elevated values under HIPAA de-identification safe                      218

                                   harbour; such values exceed 120 and are treated as missing), height 120–210 cm, weight                 219

                                   30–250 kg, and BMI 10–60; the training loader reads from the resulting precomputed index.              220

                                   Evaluation splits apply only the fixed divisor; training additionally uses light stochastic            221

                                   augmentation. The augmentation block fires with probability 0.5 per training mini-batch;               222

                                   inside it, three operations each trigger independently at probability 0.3: additive Gaussian           223

                                   noise (σ =0.02), a random integer temporal shift (uniform on [−20, +19] samples), and a                224

                                   multiplicative amplitude jitter (uniform from [0.95, 1.05]).                                           225

                                   companion.                                                                                           358

                                   correction; no confirmatory inference should be drawn from these values.                             367

                                   5.7. Methodological contribution                                                                     378


                                        The seed-matched paired-Wilcoxon measurement pipeline introduced here is a gen-                 379

                                   eralisable framework for multimodal-ML ablations. For each seed s and variant Vk , one               380




                                                                                                 https://doi.org/10.3390/math1010000
Version May 19, 2026 submitted to Mathematics                                                                                12 of 28




                                   trains to obtain a scalar evaluation metric rk,s . Pairwise incremental effects are estimated as     381

                                   δjk,s = r j,s − rk,s . Inference proceeds by applying the exact two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank       382

                                   test, BH-FDR correction over the pre-specified confirmatory test family, and percentile              383

                                   bootstrap CIs. Effect sizes are reported as Cohen’s dz with Hedges’ small-sample correction          384

                                   gz . Exploratory groups report raw p-values descriptively without FDR correction. The                385

                                   pipeline applies to any setting satisfying three conditions: (i) a baseline model can be paired      386

                                   with an augmented variant across the same random seeds; (ii) the number of seeds is small            387

                                   (say, n ∈ [5, 20]); and (iii) one seeks calibrated inference about the incremental contribution      388

                                   of an additional input modality or component.                                                        389

                                         Our 20-seed protocol yields a clear and nuanced answer. Both demographic and                                 516

                                   anthropometric variables show statistically significant gains. Demographics alone (age,                            517

                                   sex) yield ∆= + 0.0007 macro-AUC (pBH ≈0.009, dz =0.64, npos =16/20 seeds). This small                             518

                                   but reproducible effect is consistent with the partial recoverability argument of Attia et                         519

                                   al. [17]: age and sex are substantially recoverable from the waveform (sex AUC 0.97, age                           520

                                   MAE 6.9 y), yet a residual increment not fully captured by the ECG-only model remains de-                          521

                                   tectable with 20 paired seeds. The incremental anthropometric gain beyond demographics                             522

                                   is larger and more significant (∆= + 0.0012, pBH ≈0.001, dz =0.95): body-size variables pro-                       523

                                   vide complementary information not easily recoverable from the 12-lead waveform alone.                             524

                                   Both effects remain small in absolute terms (∆<0.002 macro-AUC) and should be read                                 525

                                   as protocol-specific measurements rather than as clinically large effect sizes. No clinical                        526




                                                                                                               https://doi.org/10.3390/math1010000
Version May 19, 2026 submitted to Mathematics                                                                                 16 of 28




                                   decision benefit, calibration improvement, or net-reclassification gain is claimed; whether           527

                                   these AUC increments translate to any clinical utility requires prospective evaluation on             528

                                   held-out cohorts.                                                                                     529



                                   7.2. Q2 — which class benefits from which metadata? The MI/STTC/NORM finding                          530


                                         With 20 seeds and BH-FDR correction, the class-wise effect of full metadata localises to        531

                                   three classes. MI shows the strongest effect (+0.0058, pBH <0.0001, dz =2.26), followed by            532

                                   STTC (+0.0018, pBH =0.00035) and NORM (+0.0016, pBH <0.0001). Exploratory per-class                   533

                                   tests for the demographic-only contrast (demo−none) also identify nominal significance for            534

                                   NORM (praw =0.006) and MI (praw =0.003); these tests are not in any pre-specified BH-FDR              535

                                   family and are reported descriptively. This pattern suggests that age and sex supply a useful         536

                                   prior for morphology-sensitive classes even when anthropometrics are absent. Plausible                537

                                   mechanisms include body-size-conditioned calibration of ST-segment amplitudes, Q-wave                 538

                                   depth, and related markers for MI, and thoracic electrical property effects for STTC.                 539



                                   7.3. The HYP paradox: no detectable gain at the superclass level                                      540


                                        Even with 20 seeds, HYP shows no statistically significant gain (+0.0004, pBH =0.648).           541

                                   This pattern is consistent with four explanatory hypotheses: (1) low HYP prevalence limits            542

                                   paired test power for a genuinely small effect; (2) the TSPool’s max / min voltage statistics         543

                                   already partially absorb body-habitus effects; (3) superclass heterogeneity dilutes a more            544

                                   specific LVH-related signal; and (4) HYP label noise in borderline cases attenuates any               545

                                   body-habitus signal. The non-significant HYP result with 20 seeds makes a large effect                546

                                   unlikely under this protocol, though smaller or subclass-specific effects (e.g., an LVH-              547

                                   specific signal beneath the superclass label) remain possible and were not tested here.               548

                                   Exploratory examination of the demo−none HYP test (∆= + 0.0015, praw =0.048) reveals                  549

                                   a borderline positive demographic signal that does not pass the exploratory threshold                 550

                                   of p<0.01 and is not sustained in the full-metadata DA−NONE contrast (pBH =0.648),                    551

                                   consistent with the interpretation that anthropometric variables do not amplify the marginal          552

                                   demographic effect for HYP.                                                                           553



                                   7.4. CD: demographics and the confounding role of age                                                 554


                                         The demo variant produces a slight negative trend in CD AUC (−0.0011, praw =0.123,              555

                                   exploratory), but this does not survive BH-FDR correction at 20 seeds. This remains                   556

                                   consistent with the age-as-confounder hypothesis: directly supplying age may shift the CD             557

                                   decision boundary, but the effect is not reproducibly negative. The demo+anthro CD delta              558

                                   is also non-significant (−0.0003, pBH =0.648).                                                        559



                                   7.5. Q3 — graceful degradation                                                                        560


                                         The availability-aware pathway—combining missingness indicators (Eq. (1)), zero-                561

                                   initialised residual injection (Eq. (8)), and sample meta-dropout during training—yields a            562

                                   model whose macro-AUC under full anthropometric suppression remains within ≈ 0.0014                   563

                                   of the full unmasked variant (Fig. 4), converging to the demographics-only baseline. In               564

                                   PTB-XL, qmeta saturates to 1 for nearly all records when age and sex are present (as in the           565

                                   demo and demo+anthro conditions); the primary graceful-degradation mechanism under                    566

                                   anthropometric dropout is therefore the missingness indicator encoding and sample meta-               567

                                   dropout rather than qmeta interpolation—qmeta acts mainly as a hard gate between the                  568

                                   all-metadata and no-metadata conditions. This robustness is demonstrated under synthetic              569

                                   inference-time masking; generalisability to structurally missing data under MCAR, MAR,                570

                                   or MNAR mechanisms remains an open evaluation direction.                                              571




                                                                                                  https://doi.org/10.3390/math1010000
Version May 19, 2026 submitted to Mathematics                                                                                    17 of 28




                                   7.6. Group D: augmentation should be controlled and reported                                             572


                                        The exploratory Group D result (∆=−0.0014, p=0.002, n=20) reveals that the light                    573

                                   stochastic augmentation block—Gaussian noise, temporal shift, and amplitude jitter—                      574

                                   contributes a measurable positive effect. The significant result (the only such finding                  575

                                   among all exploratory groups) suggests that augmentation should be treated as a design                   576

                                   factor to control and report rather than an optional add-on; confirmatory inference would                577

                                   require a pre-declared seed budget and FDR correction.                                                   578



                                   7.7. Q4 — comparability of earlier multimodal gains                                                      579


                                        Our 20-seed protocol reveals within-variant standard deviations of 0.0010–0.0013                    580

                                   macro-AUC, of the same order as the measured between-variant effects. The audited litera-                581

                                   ture does not provide a directly comparable PTB-XL experiment separating age/sex from                    582

                                   height/weight/BMI with repeated seeds, paired tests, and missingness stress testing. We                  583

                                   therefore position this paper as providing a narrow but statistically honest reproducibility             584

                                   baseline: +0.0007 from demographics and +0.0019 from full metadata, with class-resolved                  585

                                   and missingness-audited evidence.                                                                        586



                                   7.8. Q5 — architectural ablations: ECG is essential, gating is flexible                                  587


                                         Exploratory Group E ablations (10 seeds, uncorrected) suggest two patterns. First, the             588

                                   ECG signal is indispensable: the meta-only variant (E1) collapses to 0.6966 macro-AUC, far               589

                                   below any ECG-based variant in this study. Second, the specific fusion mechanism does                    590

                                   not drive macro-AUC differences at this scale: all six mechanism variants (E2–E7) achieve                591

                                   0.9287–0.9293, within the noise of the standard configuration (p>0.27 in all cases). This is             592

                                   notable because E2 (simple concatenation) and E3 (additive injection) achieve performance                593

                                   comparable to the GLU gate, suggesting that the gate’s added complexity is not necessary                 594

                                   to capture the metadata signal at this effect magnitude. The E8 result (0.5046 macro-AUC)                595

                                   is a distribution-shift negative control: training with zeroed metadata and then evaluating              596

                                   with real metadata induces a severe train/inference distribution mismatch. This does not                 597

                                   imply that metadata must generally be present during training; the none variant (0.9271                  598

                                   macro-AUC) demonstrates that ECG-only training without any metadata pathway remains                      599

                                   fully competitive.                                                                                       600



                                   7.9. Q6 — fold-level difficulty: fold 10 had lower absolute AUC than four alternative folds              601


                                         Group F shows that the four alternative fold pairs (folds 2, 3, 7, 8 as test folds) all            602

                                   achieve macro-AUC in the range [0.9400, 0.9445], substantially above fold 10 (0.9289). This              603

                                   0.011–0.016 gap is consistent with fold-level difficulty variation in stratified k-fold settings         604

                                   and indicates that fold 10 yields lower absolute demo+anthro macro-AUC than the other                    605

                                   tested folds. The within-fold paired comparisons of Group A are unaffected by this fold-                 606

                                   level variation; the absolute difference should be noted when positioning fold-10 results                607

                                   relative to benchmarks evaluated on averaged or best-of-fold metrics.                                    608



                                   7.10. Sensitivity ablations: no detectable sensitivity to hyperparameters                                609


                                         The exploratory multi-seed sensitivity analyses (Groups B and C, Section 6.7) are                  610

                                   consistent with the reported results not being sensitive to a specific metadata hidden size              611

                                   or AUC-surrogate weight. All seven non-reference variants (three metaH, four lauc) show                  612

                                   Wilcoxon p>0.32 against the standard configuration, with macro-AUC ranges spanning                       613

                                   less than 0.0012; no confirmatory inference is drawn from these exploratory results.                     614



                                   7.11. Why small measured effects remain informative                                                      615


                                        Reporting +0.0007 and +0.0019 macro-AUC gains is a refinement of the multimodal                     616

                                   claim rather than a null result. The paired seed-level 95% CIs are strictly positive for the full-       617




                                                                                                    https://doi.org/10.3390/math1010000
Version May 19, 2026 submitted to Mathematics                                                                               18 of 28




                                   metadata contrast ([0.0014, 0.0023]), supporting a reproducible directional effect without          618

                                   implying a large clinical effect size. The demographic-only CI ([0.0002, 0.0011]) is positive       619

                                   but narrow, consistent with a small real effect that required 20 seeds to detect. These results     620

                                   indicate that: (i) a strong ECG-only model is already close to the incremental information          621

                                   supplied by the tested metadata; (ii) the residual signal localises to morphology-rich classes      622

                                   (MI, STTC, NORM); (iii) demographic fusion alone can create class-specific shifts auditable         623

                                   only through paired multi-seed statistics; and (iv) anthropometrics contribute a second,            624

                                   statistically significant increment beyond demographics.                                            625



                                   7.12. Limitations                                                                                   626


                             1.        Evaluation scope and fold difficulty. The confirmatory Group A results are conditioned          627

                                       on fold 10, which Group F identifies as lying below the inter-fold demo+anthro macro-           628

                                       AUC range [0.9400, 0.9445] by approximately 0.011–0.016 for absolute performance.               629

                                       This indicates that fold 10 yields lower absolute full-metadata AUC; the within-fold            630

                                       paired variant comparisons remain valid. No external cohort evaluation has been                 631

                                       performed, limiting generalisation across institutions, acquisition devices, demographic        632

                                       distributions, and temporal shifts.                                                             633

                             2.        Task and metadata scope. The analysis is restricted to the 5-superclass task and to             634

                                       age, sex, height, weight, and BMI. Variables such as blood pressure, electrolytes, and          635

                                       echocardiographic measurements are absent. Age, sex, and body size are not purely               636

                                       biological descriptors—they also carry population-structure signal—so measured gains            637

                                       cannot be unambiguously attributed to physiological mechanisms alone.                           638

                             3.        Model selection and downstream metrics. Class-wise F1 thresholds and checkpoint                 639

                                       selection are all determined on fold 9 and fixed for fold 10. The reported p-values are         640

                                       therefore conditional on this validation-based selection protocol, and F1 should be read        641

                                       more cautiously than AUC.                                                                       642

                             4.        Architectural attribution. Although Group E ablations (E2–E7) show that individual              643

                                       components do not materially affect macro-AUC, the entangled nature of certain hyper-           644

                                       parameters (e.g., meta_hid in Group B controls both projection and gate dimensions)             645

                                       prevents clean attribution. The metadata MLPs retain BatchNorm, which may be less               646

                                       stable than layer-normalised alternatives when metadata-rich samples are rare within a          647

                                       mini-batch.                                                                                     648

                             5.        Statistical granularity. The reported bootstrap intervals quantify variability across           649

                                       seed-level runs rather than patient-level resampling, and the seed-matched comparisons          650

                                       share the same dataset, preprocessing pipeline, and split definition. The paired contrasts      651

                                       are therefore correlated repeated runs on one fixed cohort split rather than independent        652

                                       cohort replications. Exploratory group results (B, C, D, E) report raw p-values without         653

                                       FDR correction and should not support confirmatory conclusions.                                 654

                             6.        Reproducibility provenance. All 250 runs were executed on a CPU-only machine (Intel             655

                                       Core i5, 8 GB RAM) using PyTorch 2.3.1+cpu in a local Jupyter Notebook environment              656

                                       under Windows. CPU-only execution eliminates one source of non-determinism (CUDA                657

                                       parallel atomic operations); results may nonetheless differ across different CPU archi-         658

                                       tectures, BLAS library versions, or threading configurations, so identical hardware and         659

                                       software environment is required for bitwise reproduction.                                      660

                                         EZNX-ATLAS-A reaches macro-AUC comparable to the canonical PTB-XL superclass                  662

                                   benchmark while serving as an instrument for measuring the incremental contribution of              663

                                   clinical metadata across a 250-run campaign organised in six experimental groups (A–F).             664

                                   (a)      Demographic variables (age, sex) add a small but statistically significant gain              666

                                            (∆= + 0.0007 macro-AUC, 95% paired seed-level CI [0.0002, 0.0011], pBH ≈0.009);              667

                                            the effect localises to NORM and MI and is consistent with a residual demographic            668

                                            significant gain of +0.0012 over demographics (pBH ≈0.001), for a total full-metadata        671

                                            sistent with voltage-based LVH criteria being diluted by superclass heterogeneity,           675

                                            hypothesis.                                                                                  677

                                   (d)      The availability-aware pathway—combining missingness indicators, zero-initialised            678
